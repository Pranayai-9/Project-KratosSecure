Autonomous AI agents are increasingly capable of accessing organizational data, invoking tools and performing multi-step tasks. In healthcare environments, such autonomy introduces risks involving patient-data confidentiality, authorization, record integrity, financial manipulation, privilege escalation and unsafe operational decisions. Existing AI safety evaluations often focus on model responses rather than behavior across realistic, stateful environments. This project proposes a controlled healthcare digital twin in which an autonomous hospital-support agent performs legitimate operational tasks while an adaptive adversarial agent generates controlled security challenges. The system observes the target agent's decisions, tool usage, authorization attempts, data access and recovery behavior to evaluate its behavioral resilience.
